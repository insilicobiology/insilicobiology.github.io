About the Hub

Mathematics is biology's next microscope, only better; biology is mathematics' next physics, only better. At In Silico Biology, we focus on developing robust computational architectures, interpretable machine learning frameworks (like UbiQTree), and scalable pipelines for modern bioinformatics challenges.

Whether exploring multi-omics datasets, clinical machine learning models, or uncertainty quantification in tree-based algorithms, the goal remains the same: making data-driven biology reliable and open to the scientific community.
