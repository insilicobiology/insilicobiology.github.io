About Me

I am a computational biologist working at the intersection of machine learning, public health, and data science. Currently based at the Robert Koch Institute in Berlin, my day-to-day work involves exploring how advanced computational models can help monitor, understand, and improve public health outcomes.

Beyond institutional research, I enjoy developing open-source libraries that bridge the gap between heavy mathematical theory and everyday biological workflows—making advanced computational biology transparent, robust, and accessible.
